# brandkit.md

## Brand Overview

AGI With AI is a modern, startup-focused development agency. The visual identity should feel clean, sharp, and slightly bold with subtle neo-brutalism influences—without being overwhelming.

---

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors

* **Black (Headings & Key Elements)**
  `#111111`

* **Soft White (Main Background)**
  `#FAFAFA`

* **Warm Light Yellow (Accent Background Sections)**
  `#FFF4CC`

---

### Secondary Colors

* **Light Grey (Sections / Cards Background)**
  `#F2F2F2`

* **Border Grey (Subtle separation)**
  `#D9D9D9`

---

### Text Colors

* **Primary Text (Headings):** `#111111`
* **Body Text:** `#333333`
* **Muted Text:** `#666666`

---

## Typography

### Font Family

* Primary: `Inter` (or system-ui fallback)
* Fallback: `Arial, sans-serif`

---

### Font Weights

* Headings: 600–700
* Body: 400–500
* Buttons: 500–600

---

### Font Sizes (Desktop)

* H1: 48px
* H2: 36px
* H3: 28px
* H4: 22px
* Body Large: 18px
* Body: 16px
* Small Text: 14px

---

### Font Sizes (Mobile)

* H1: 32px
* H2: 26px
* H3: 22px
* H4: 18px
* Body Large: 16px
* Body: 15px
* Small Text: 13px

---

### Line Height

* Headings: 1.2 – 1.3
* Body: 1.5 – 1.7

---

### Letter Spacing

* Headings: -0.5px to 0px
* Body: 0px to 0.2px

---

## Layout & Spacing

### Container Width

* Max Width: 1200px
* Padding (Desktop): 24px–32px
* Padding (Mobile): 16px–20px

---

### Section Spacing

* Desktop: 80px–100px vertical
* Mobile: 50px–70px vertical

---

### Grid System

* 12-column grid (desktop)
* Single column / stacked (mobile)

---

## Buttons

### Style

* Background: Black `#111111`
* Text: White `#FFFFFF`
* Border: 2px solid black
* Border Radius: 6px (slightly sharp, not too rounded)

### Hover

* Background: White
* Text: Black
* Border remains

---

## Borders (Neo-Brutalism Touch)

* Use **2px solid black borders** for:

  * Cards
  * Buttons
  * Input fields

* Avoid shadows or keep them minimal

* Slight offset or sharp edges preferred over soft UI

---

## Cards & Components

* Background: White or Light Grey `#F2F2F2`
* Border: 2px solid black
* Padding: 20px–24px
* No heavy gradients or glass effects

---

## Forms & Inputs

* Border: 2px solid black
* Padding: 12px–14px
* Font Size: 16px
* Focus State:

  * Border stays black
  * Optional subtle background highlight (`#FFF4CC`)

---

## Icons & Visual Style

* Simple, outline or minimal filled icons
* Black or dark grey only
* No colorful icon packs

---

## Imagery

* Use clean UI mockups
* White or neutral backgrounds
* No heavy overlays or gradients

---

## Tone & UI Feel

* Clean and structured
* Slightly bold due to borders
* Startup-friendly, not corporate-heavy
* Minimal distractions, high clarity

---

## Do Not Use

* Gradients
* Bright neon colors (especially blue)
* Over-rounded elements
* Heavy shadows
* Cluttered layouts

---

## Summary

The design should feel:

* Clean like modern SaaS
* Bold like neo-brutalism (light touch only)
* Simple, readable, and conversion-focused

Consistency across spacing, typography, and borders is critical.
